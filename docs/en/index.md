## Choose a task <span id="选择你要做的事"></span> {#choose-a-task}

### Install or configure the Chinese localization client <span id="安装或配置汉化客户端"></span> {#install-or-configure-the-chinese-localization-client}

Choose your device in the [platform overview](/en/platform/). Then follow [Usage and Configuration](/en/guide/) to enable translated text, Chinese main story voices, or localized images and videos.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Localization features are disabled on first launch. Configure them through the control panel in the in-game notices or in your browser. These features provide Chinese localization; the English documentation does not add English game resources.

### Manage the JP game on your computer <span id="在电脑上管理日服游戏"></span> {#manage-the-jp-game-on-your-computer}

[Cafe Launcher](/en/cafe-launcher/) is a third-party desktop launcher built with .NET and Avalonia. It installs, updates, repairs, and launches the game, with Official and Cafe download sources.

Windows x64 is officially supported. Experimental builds are available for macOS Apple Silicon and Linux x64. See [Installation and First Run](/en/cafe-launcher/installation) for packages and platform limitations.

## Get help <span id="获取帮助"></span> {#get-help}

- Localization not applied, broken resources, or a client update request: see the [localization FAQ](/en/guide/qa).
- Launcher download, verification, settings, or update errors: see the [Cafe Launcher FAQ](/en/cafe-launcher/faq).
- Still stuck: prepare your version, reproduction steps, and logs using the [Feedback Guide](/en/cafe-launcher/feedback).
